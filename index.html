Accessible charts with Highcharts and the ColorADD system

Three Highcharts examples — a bar chart, a donut chart, and a line chart — where every color also carries a ColorADD symbol. Color is never the only cue. Each chart is paired with the same data as a table, and every series is directly labeled.

How this meets the brief

  • Series distinguished by color, ColorADD symbol, and direct label — three independent, non-color-only cues.
  • Every chart ships with an adjacent data table so the numbers are readable without seeing the graphic.
  • All text pairs at 4.5:1+; chart segments, borders, and focus rings at 3:1+.
  • Full keyboard navigation via the Highcharts accessibility module. Try Tab, then arrow keys inside a chart.
  • Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion. Chart entrance animation is disabled when the OS asks for it.
  • Built to the AI A11y Toolkit baseline — WCAG 2.2 AA target.

ColorADD key

ColorADD is a symbol system by Miguel Neiva that lets people with color vision deficiency identify colors when color alone would otherwise be the only cue (coloradd.net). Three primary shapes represent blue, red, and yellow. Secondary colors combine those primaries in one tile. A shape enclosed in an outlined box indicates a lighter shade of that color; a shape cut out of a filled box indicates a darker shade. Below is the key for every color used in the charts on this page.

  • Light blue — blue wedge inside an outlined box (the box is the light-shade indicator)
  • Blue — a wedge pointing to the top-left corner of the tile
  • Dark blue — blue wedge cut out of a filled box (the filled box is the dark-shade indicator)
  • Orange — red wedge combined with the yellow diagonal bar (red + yellow)
  • Red — a wedge pointing to the bottom-right corner of the tile
  • Purple — the blue and red wedges combined into a single shape (red + blue)

Yellow appears in ColorADD as a diagonal bar running from the top-right to the bottom-left of the tile. It has no color of its own in this palette; it shows up as a component of the orange glyph. Glyph geometry follows the official ColorADD reference. In production, license the official assets from coloradd.net.
