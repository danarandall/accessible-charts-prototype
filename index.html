Accessible charts with Highcharts and the ColorADD system

Two Highcharts examples — a bar chart and a donut chart — where every color also carries a ColorADD symbol. Color is never the only cue. Each chart is paired with the same data as a table, and every series is directly labeled.

How this meets the brief

  • Series distinguished by color, ColorADD symbol, direct label, and pattern — four independent cues.
  • Every chart ships with an adjacent data table so the numbers are readable without seeing the graphic.
  • All text pairs at 4.5:1+; chart segments, borders, and focus rings at 3:1+.
  • Full keyboard navigation via the Highcharts accessibility module. Try Tab, then arrow keys inside a chart.
  • Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion. Chart entrance animation is disabled when the OS asks for it.
  • Built to the AI A11y Toolkit baseline — WCAG 2.2 AA target.

ColorADD key

ColorADD is a symbol system by Miguel Neiva that lets people with color vision deficiency identify colors when color alone is a factor of identification, orientation, or choice (overview). Three primary symbols represent blue, yellow, and red. Secondary colors combine those symbols. Black and white fill or invert the swatch to indicate dark or light tones.

  • Blue — upward triangle, right
  • Yellow — downward triangle, left
  • Red — diagonal line
  • Green — blue + yellow
  • Orange — red + yellow
  • Purple — red + blue
  • Brown — red + green
  • Black — filled square
  • White — empty square

The symbols shown here follow the widely documented ColorADD forms (Wikipedia, EIB). In production, license the official assets from coloradd.net.

What still needs human and screen reader testing

  • Confirm the ColorADD symbols in each series marker render at ≥ 3:1 against the fill.
  • Confirm the license status of the ColorADD marks before production use.
  • Run an automated engine (IBM Equal Access or Pa11y) against the built page and fix findings at the source.
